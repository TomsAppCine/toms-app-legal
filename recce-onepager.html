01 — Privacy

What happens to your data

Data Not Collected

TOM'S APP has no servers and no accounts. Everything the app does happens on your device.

TOM'S APP is a measuring instrument, not an online service. It doesn't ask you to sign in, it doesn't create a profile, and it never sends your information to us or to anyone else. We — the developers — receive nothing, because there is nothing sent.

What the app uses on your device, and why:

  • Camera — to show the live viewfinder and overlays, and to save your reference photos. Frames are processed on the device and are never transmitted.
  • Location (GPS) — to calculate the sun's position for your exact spot. Your coordinates stay on the device and are used only for that calculation.
  • Photos — to save the images you capture into your own photo library. The app writes to your library; it does not read, upload, or share it.
  • Motion sensors — to read the phone's tilt and orientation so the horizon and sun arc line up with the real world.

A note on photos and location metadata. Reference photos you capture may include the GPS location where you took them, saved inside the image file (EXIF), because knowing the spot is the point of a scout. Once you share a photo — by AirDrop, WhatsApp, email, or backing it up to a cloud drive — that location travels with it, and what happens to it is then governed by whichever service you used, not by TOM'S APP. TOM'S APP contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party code. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to sell, share, lose, or hand over.

03 — FAQ

When it looks broken but isn't

TOM'S APP is an instrument, so some of its honest behaviour can look like a fault. The common ones:

The sun arc won't show up indoors +

Sun Path needs a GPS fix, and GPS struggles inside buildings. Step near a window or outside and give it a moment to lock on. No signal means no calculation — the app waits rather than guessing.

The compass heading drifts or feels wrong +

Phone compasses are thrown off by steel, cars, rebar, and street canyons — exactly the environments you scout in. Use the tap-the-sun calibration: point at the real sun and tap it, and TOM'S APP re-aligns itself in one move.

Why did it save a second, blank-looking photo? +

That's the RAW capture — a clean, unmarked exposure plate saved alongside your annotated reference. It isn't blank; it just has no overlays drawn on it, so you keep a pristine original. [Adjust if you add a RAW on/off toggle.]

Do I need to sign in or make an account? +

No. TOM'S APP works entirely on your device. There's nothing to log into and nothing stored on a server — you pick it up and it works, like a light meter.

Does it work without internet? +

Yes — that's the whole idea. TOM'S APP needs no connection to do its job. GPS and the sun calculations work offline, wherever you are.

Why is the app locked to landscape? +

Because you frame in landscape. TOM'S APP is built around the way a camera is held, so the viewfinder, arc, and readouts stay where your eye expects them.
